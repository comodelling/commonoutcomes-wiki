# Translating change

*Changes* on CommonOutcomes would benefit from being translated into different languages.
This would:

- make their content accessible to more people, whether within or outside the change's *scope*.
- foster influence and synergies between regions, breaking language barriers.


Although *scopes* may have their default associated language(s), a *change* may be translated in many more languages.
Translations would refer to the same *change* object, with the same *scope* and meaning.
In that sense, translations on CommonOutcomes would be actual translations, not [localisations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Language_localisation) like on Wikipedia.
