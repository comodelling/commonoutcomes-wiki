Welcome to CommonOutcomes' wiki

This wiki provides some context, advice and directions for the CommonOutcomes platform.
It also serves as a working reference for the project, and will thus co-evolve both with the platform and its parent software project, CommonGraph.

beta testing

We are currently looking for volunteers to beta test CommonOutcomes. If you are interested, please contact us. Until the end of this phase, the platform will be accessible only to logged-in users.

Beyond the different ways to collaborate we suggest in this section, we warmly welcome any additional feedback or questions you may have via email.
